## The one thing to keep in mind

At **Step 1 the panel reads B1 only** — 5 pages of synopsis plus 4 pages of CV. B2 is not
opened. Roughly three quarters of proposals are rejected on that basis alone. So B1 is not a
summary of B2; it is the proposal. Budget your effort accordingly: B1 should be finished and
externally read before B2 is drafted in earnest.

## Verify before relying on

Figures here were assembled from ERC and university research-office sources in August 2026.

- ERC Work Programme 2027 (Commission Decision C(2026)4907)
