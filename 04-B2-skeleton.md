# Part B2 — Scientific proposal (7 pages)

Read only at **Step 2**, by the panel plus external remote referees who *are* in your subfield.
Different audience from B1: here, technical depth is expected and vagueness is punished.

B2 must be readable standalone, but must not contradict or restructure B1. The objectives keep
the same numbers and the same names throughout.

## Structure

### 1. Methodology (~3.5 pp)

Organised by work package, one WP per objective plus usually a small management/dissemination WP.

For each WP:
- Objective it serves (same number as B1)
- Rationale in two sentences
- Experimental design — real detail: model systems, sample sizes, controls, statistical approach
- Expected outcomes, and interpretation of both positive and negative results
- Deliverables and the month they land

The remote referees are subfield experts. They will notice absent controls, underpowered designs,
and model systems that cannot support the claim. Sample size justification and a statistical plan
are not padding here — in LS panels their absence is a standard criticism.

### 2. Work plan and timeline (~1 p)

A Gantt chart, 60 months, WPs against time, with milestones and decision points marked. Show
where objectives run in parallel — this is where you visually prove they are not serially
dependent.

Mark **go/no-go decision points** explicitly. They demonstrate that risk is managed rather than
merely acknowledged.

### 3. Risks and mitigation (~0.75 p)

A table: risk · likelihood · impact · mitigation/fallback. Include the genuinely serious risks.
A risk table containing only trivia ("staff recruitment may be delayed") tells a referee you have
not thought about the science.

Each fallback should be a real alternative experiment, not "we will optimise conditions".

### 4. Resources and budget justification (~1.5 pp)

- **Personnel**: each position, its role, which WPs, and for how long. Justify by the work, not
  by the money available. Your own committed time as PI must be stated and must be credible —
  for StG, a minimum share of working time is required and it is checked.
- **Equipment**: justified by the experiments, with a note on what the host already provides.
- **Consumables**: costed against the actual experiments, at a level a referee can sanity-check.
- **Travel, publication, other**: brief.
- **Additional funding** (start-up/equipment/relocation), if claimed: justify separately and
  concretely.

The budget is a statement about whether you can run a group. A €1.5M request with three postdocs
and no technician, or with equipment nobody will operate, reads as inexperience.
