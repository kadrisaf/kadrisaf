# ERC-2027-StG — call facts

## Dates

| | |
|---|---|
| Call opened | 22 July 2026 |
| **Deadline** | **14 October 2026, 17:00:00 Brussels time** |
| Step 1 outcome | ~April 2027 |
| Step 2 interviews | ~mid 2027 |
| Step 2 outcome | ~August 2027 |
| Earliest project start | late 2027 / early 2028 |

The portal does not accept late submissions for any reason, and it slows badly in the final
48 hours. Treat **12 October** as the real deadline.

## Money

- Up to **€1.5M** over **5 years** (the core grant).
- Up to **€1M additional** for start-up costs, major equipment, or access to large facilities.
- Up to **€2M additional** instead, if you are relocating to the EU/Associated Country from
  outside it.
- Call budget ~€594M for an estimated ~373 grants.

Additional funding is not free money — it must be justified by the project's needs, and asking
for equipment you have not justified reads as inexperience. It is also not counted against you
if you don't ask.

## Eligibility — the 2027 change

**PhD awarded 0–10 years before 1 January 2027**, i.e. defence between **1 Jan 2017 and
31 Dec 2026**. (Previously 2–7 years. This is the single biggest change in WP2027.)

The reference is normally the **date of successful defence**, not the date of the certificate —
but the host institution's research office should confirm which date your certificate supports,
because the certificate is an obligatory annex.

**Extensions** to the window are granted for documented cases:

- Maternity: typically 18 months per child (or actual leave taken, if longer).
- Paternity/parental leave: actual leave taken.
- Long-term illness, national service, clinical training.
- WP2027 adds extensions for applicants who have experienced gender-based or other violence.

Extensions must be declared and evidenced in Part A. Claim everything you are entitled to —
it is a stated right, not special pleading, and it also shifts how the panel reads your output
per year.

## New restrictions in WP2027 — read carefully

1. **One proposal only** across the entire ERC Work Programme 2027. You cannot hedge across
   StG and another scheme. (A proposal declared ineligible does not consume this.)
2. **StG/CoG once per career.** Holding either one means you can never hold that scheme again.
3. **Resubmission blocks tightened.** Poor Step 1 outcomes in recent calls bar you from
   applying for a defined period, and the blocking periods were each extended by one year for
   2027. *If you have submitted an ERC proposal before, tell me the call and the letter grade —
   this is the first thing to check, before any writing.*

## The 5–10 year overlap: StG or CoG?

Years 5–10 post-PhD now qualify for both. StG is more money-efficient to win (higher success
rate, lower expectations of an established group); CoG carries more money (€2M) and expects a
consolidated independent record. If you are in the overlap, the deciding question is not your
years but **whether you already lead an independent group with independent output**. If not,
StG — and the panel will read you against people at your stage, which is now a much wider
range than it used to be. Expect the StG pool in 2027 to be more competitive than historically,
because seven-plus-year postdocs who used to be pushed to CoG can now come here.

## Evaluation

**Sole criterion: scientific excellence.** Nothing else — not societal impact, not consortium
balance, not national quotas. Two elements, weighted equally:

**The research project**
- Ground-breaking nature and potential impact
- Scientific ambition, and whether the objectives are beyond the state of the art
- Feasibility, and whether the risk is justified by the gain

**The Principal Investigator**
- Intellectual capacity and creativity
- Commitment to the project (time, and that it is genuinely yours)

**Step 1**: panel members only, reading **B1 + CV/track record**. B2 is not opened.
**Step 2**: panel plus external remote referees, reading everything, plus an **interview**
(~10 min presentation + ~15–20 min questions, in Brussels or remote).

## Structure of the proposal

| Part | Content | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| A | Administrative forms, PI data, host, eligibility declarations | portal forms |
| B1 | Extended synopsis: state of the art, objectives, overall strategy, impact | **5 pages** |
| B1 | CV and track record (incl. career breaks, broader outputs) | **4 pages** |
| B2 | Methodology, work plan, risks and mitigation, budget justification | **7 pages** |

Page limits are enforced mechanically — excess pages are blanked before the panel sees them.
Font and margin minimums are specified in the Information for Applicants; do not shave them.

Obligatory annexes: **PhD certificate**, **host institution support letter**. Both take longer
to obtain than you expect. Start them in week 1.

Panel choice is a strategic decision, not a filing decision — it determines the ~12–15 people
who read your B1 and whether any of them is close enough to your field to champion it. Confirm
the exact 2027 panel titles and descriptors in the Work Programme before choosing, and check
each candidate panel's published membership from recent calls.
